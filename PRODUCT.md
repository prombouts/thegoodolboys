## Users

Primary audience is inferred from the existing site: people in and around 's-Hertogenbosch who want to discover The Good Ol' Boys, plus venue, cafe, festival, or event organizers evaluating the band for a live booking. The specific priority between listeners and bookers is open for confirmation.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces The Good Ol' Boys as an acoustic cover band and gives visitors enough evidence to decide whether to attend a performance or make contact about a booking. It supports discovery through the band's story, repertoire, upcoming and past performances, photographs, promo video, Instagram, and contact form. The desired primary success action is inferred as a completed contact or booking inquiry; the preferred success metric is open for confirmation.

## Positioning

The band's distinct position is inferred from the current copy: recognizable americana, country, blues, and related classics performed acoustically by two guitar-and-vocal players and a bassist, with a warm local presence around 's-Hertogenbosch. Future work must not strengthen this into unsupported claims about scale, clientele, pricing, or availability.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the site on mobile or desktop, often while deciding on a cafe, festival, or outdoor event. They may scan the repertoire, check dates and venues, watch the bierfestival compilation, inspect live photos, follow Instagram, or open the external Google contact form. Performance dates and venue information are time-sensitive and must remain easy to update.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current experience is a single-page static site with anchored navigation.
- It includes responsive navigation, scroll reveals, a gallery lightbox with keyboard and touch navigation, an upcoming-performance countdown, external venue links, an Instagram link, and an external contact form.
- Current public terminology and copy are Dutch, with music genres and song titles retained as proper names.
- Existing logo, member portraits, gallery photographs, promo video, favicon, and Instagram/contact destinations are real project assets and should be preserved unless explicitly replaced.
- Do not invent performances, testimonials, clients, audience numbers, pricing, availability, or other proof.
- Exact booking workflow, event-data ownership, and whether a privacy/cookie statement is required remain open decisions.
